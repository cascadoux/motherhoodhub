Why this exists

The other half of the family

The Lingram Group already runs the Fatherhood Engagement Hub, helping fathers reconnect with their children and navigate child welfare systems. The work made something obvious: a child's outcome rarely turns on one parent alone.

Mothers in these systems carry a particular weight — assumed to cope, judged more harshly when they struggle, and often the last to be offered support rather than instructions. This Hub is the deliberate counterpart: the same confidential, practical approach, designed for mothers.

You should not have to become an expert in bureaucracy to keep your family together.

We are a community support organisation, not a law firm or a government agency. We help you understand, prepare and stay standing. For legal advice, you need a licensed attorney — and we will say so every time rather than guess on your behalf.

Safety first

If you are in danger right now, do not wait for us

This website is not an emergency service and is not monitored around the clock. If you or your children are unsafe, contact emergency services or one of these lines. They are staffed by trained people, free, and confidential.

  • Emergency (US): 911
  • National Domestic Violence Hotline (US): 1-800-799-7233, or text START to 88788
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (US): call or text 988
  • Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline (US): 1-800-422-4453

If someone monitors your devices, consider using a phone or computer they cannot access, and clearing your browsing history after visiting sites like this one.
